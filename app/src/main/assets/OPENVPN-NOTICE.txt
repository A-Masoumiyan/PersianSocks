PersianSocks bundles the OpenVPN 2.7.8 executable (GPL-2.0 with its OpenSSL linking exception), OpenSSL 3.5.9 (Apache-2.0), LZO 2.10 (GPL-2.0-or-later) and LZ4 1.10.0 (BSD-2-Clause). Copyright and license notices are included in assets/licenses. The OpenVPN program runs as a separate process. The Android build overlay/scripts are from gxosty/openvpn-android-prebuilt v2.7.8-1 (MIT); sources: https://github.com/gxosty/openvpn-android-prebuilt/tree/v2.7.8-1 . Unmodified OpenVPN source commit: dc1f569087135894ac2703aeabd7a960ee9452d4 .
Complete corresponding sources including dependency sources and build scripts accompany the APK as PersianSocks-OpenVPN-corresponding-source.tar.gz at https://github.com/A-Masoumiyan/PersianSocks/releases/tag/v0.31.0 . This archive is also reproducibly packaged by scripts/package-openvpn-source.py with pinned hashes in scripts/openvpn-source-inputs.json .
VPN Gate list: https://www.vpngate.net/api/iphone/ ; fallback directory: https://github.com/GeorgeXie2333/vpngate-list-mirror . Directory availability and advertised scores are not a verified VPN connection. Port scans verify TCP reachability only.
